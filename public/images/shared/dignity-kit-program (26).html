About the Program

500 Backpacks. 500 Young People.
One Powerful Message.

Teen Health is assembling and distributing dignity kits to youth experiencing homelessness across Los Angeles — combining immediate nourishment with a lasting sense of care.

Each backpack is filled with healthy snacks, hydration, and essential supplies — everything needed to face the day with a little more stability. These aren't generic care packages. They're thoughtfully curated kits designed to meet real, immediate needs.

Each backpack costs just $25 to assemble but delivers $50 in retail value through donated and discounted products — effectively doubling every dollar you give.

For youth without consistent access to basic essentials, a dignity kit provides more than supplies. It delivers a message: you matter, and someone sees you.

This program is part of Teen Health's broader commitment to meeting young people where they are — providing tangible, immediate support that creates the stability needed to engage with longer-term services and resources.

Eligible for corporate matching gifts. Supports youth development, homelessness services, and food security initiatives.

Distribution is coordinated directly with partners across Los Angeles to reach the youth who need it most, where they already are.
