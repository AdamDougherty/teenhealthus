Welcome!

This is your tool for editing the Teen Health website. Just talk to the AI — it does the hard part for you.

How It Works

  1. You type what you want changed in plain English
  2. The AI makes the changes for you
  3. You check your browser to see if you like it
  4. If you like it → keep going or make it live
  5. If you don't → say undo that and it's reversed

Getting Started Each Session

  1. Antigravity (the AI assistant) should already be open — you'll see the chat window on screen
  2. Open your browser to localhost:3000 — this is your live preview of the website
  3. Type what you want changed in the Antigravity chat — and check your browser to see the result!

Example Things You Can Ask

Change the hero headline to 'Nourishing youth in Orange County'

Make the donate button bigger

Update the phone number on the contact page

Add a new team member photo — I put the image in the project folder

Change the background color of the programs section to light blue

Don't Like a Change?

No worries — just say any of these:

undo that   go back   revert   I don't like that

The AI will reverse the last change. Check your browser to confirm it's back to how it was.

Ready to Publish?

When you've previewed everything and it looks good, just say:

"make it live"

This pushes your changes to the real website. Always preview first!

Never say "make it live" without checking localhost:3000 first. Always preview!

Helpful Tips

  • Keep your browser open at all times — changes appear there instantly
  • Be specific about what you want: "make the text bigger" is okay, but "make the headline on the homepage 20% bigger" is better
  • You can ask the AI questions too — "what pages does this site have?" or "where is the donate button?"
  • If something looks broken, say undo that — it's always safe
  • You cannot break anything permanently — every change is saved and can be undone
You don't need to understand code. Just describe what you want and the AI handles the rest.
